• MF Technologies Customer Repair Waiver

    1. This waiver serves to inform both the organization and its technicians of the issues brought to us by the consumer/customer. All issues are to be confirmed by the staff PRIOR to any repair or before the device is opened. If the device is in a non-responsive or non-testable state where other issues cannot be confirmed, we WILL NOT be held liable for said issues. Any repair that is specifically requested, such as 'screen repair', will have that repair done ONLY. Not all damage can be seen with the naked eye. If other obvious damages are found in the course of the repair (power button not functioning, home button sticking, no wi-fi, etc.), those damages will be assessed but NOT REPAIRED! This ensures that the repairs will be done on a timely basis and your quote remains accurate. Customers will, however, be informed of any encountered issues to ensure that they wish to continue with the repair/service before continuation.

    2. Water Damaged devices can succumb to other issues before, during and after the repair at any given time. Devices are NOT WATERPROOF BUT WATER RESISTANT; therefore, they are all susceptible to water damage. Of course, once noticed, the customer will be notified, and if not present, a photo will be taken to show evidence of such issue, and a notice will be added to the waiver to ensure that the customer and staff have been notified of said issue. Any issues associated with a water-damaged device will not be the responsibility of the organization. Only if the item that changed has a manufacturing defect that can be confirmed will it be exchanged.

    3. In the event that an issue is found with your device after a repair, the customer is to bring the device to the branch it was repaired at. In the event that a customer carries the device to another establishment to check out their device, the warranty given on the service/repair will IMMEDIATELY be voided.

    4. Any issues found with the device after repairs, a 30 Second Video recording of the issue must be sent to the WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram pages of the organization during the warranty period. This is to keep a record of the issue in order to facilitate replacement by the supplier in the event of a manufacturing defect. If the issue is related to anything outside of a manufacturing defect, it will not be covered by the warranty.

    5. Parts we INSTALL are warranty guaranteed! If we replace a part, and that part still doesn't function or experiences some manufacturing defect, we will order another replacement at our own expense. This, however, may require additional time for the part to arrive and to be installed. The device is requested once an issue/defect is found, to be returned to the branch and to be kept until assorted. Customers will be given a time frame as to how long the replacement will take, which can vary between the same day and 1 to 5 business days (EXCLUDING SATURDAY), unless the part needs to be ordered. Any other damages are the responsibility of the customer. In the event that the customer chooses to keep their device whilst awaiting the part to be replaced, any physical damage or water damage from here forward until the replacement is received is held at the responsibility of the customer, and the warranty will be considered voided.

    6. If your device has a passcode or password, we need to know this! If a device is locked, we will not be able to properly test the device once the repair is completed. If the repair is completed. and still no passcode/password is provided, the repair will be considered AS-IS! See #1 for further information, as the device will be considered in a non-responsive or non-testable state.

    7. All jobs will be held for 10 days from the date of contact that the work is completed. After the allotted time has passed, the part will be reclaimed or may be used for other purposes if warranted. After 90 days, we will attempt final contact to resolve any issues and have the device repaired and paid for. If no resolution is available or we cannot contact you at the options provided to us (disconnected number, etc.), the organization reserves the right to assume ownership of the device. We will then attempt to finalize payment in some other way, including but not limited to selling the device.

    8. If your device is repaired and you notice something not working correctly, not staying in place or peeling, or adhesive coming loose, bring the device in IMMEDIATELY. If something is coming apart or detached, DO NOT USE THE DEVICE AS NORMAL! If the adhesive is coming loose, we can fix that easily. If you decide to continue using the device as-is, any damage accrued from that will be the responsibility of the owner. Example, if, however, the screen cracks or stops functioning, the part WILL NOT BE WARRANTIED! Such events are included in our 10 Day Labor warranty. Basically, if I can't send the part back to the manufacturer on warranty, then we cannot accept it as such. We have no problem accepting responsibility for our decisions and actions, provided our customers give us the same courtesy.

    9. By signing, I hereby acknowledge the provided statements of service and warranty.
